Horse

NutritionGood-quality forage as the dietary base, with concentrates according to workload, plus minerals and water.
Housing / EnvironmentSafe turnout, clean stable, ventilation, exercise and regular hoof care.
Preventive Veterinary CareDental examinations, farriery, vaccination, parasite control and conditioning.
Common Health ConcernsColic, lameness, dental problems, respiratory disease and skin disorders.
Typical PurposeRiding, sport, work and breeding depending on breed
ImportantBreed needs can vary by age, sex, workload, climate and individual health status.

For illness, injury, breathing difficulty, severe diarrhea/vomiting, inability to eat/drink, collapse or other urgent signs, contact a veterinarian promptly.
