Buffalo

NutritionGreen and dry fodder, silage, concentrates, minerals and clean water.
Housing / EnvironmentShade, ventilation, clean resting areas and access to cooling/wallowing where appropriate.
Preventive Veterinary CareVaccination, mastitis prevention, hoof care, parasite control and reproductive monitoring.
Common Health ConcernsMastitis, parasites, heat stress, metabolic and reproductive disorders.
Typical PurposeMilk, breeding and agricultural production
ImportantBreed needs can vary by age, sex, workload, climate and individual health status.

For illness, injury, breathing difficulty, severe diarrhea/vomiting, inability to eat/drink, collapse or other urgent signs, contact a veterinarian promptly.
