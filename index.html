🐕 Dog

One of the most popular companion animals worldwide.

Popular Breeds

Labrador Retriever, German Shepherd, Golden Retriever, French Bulldog, Bulldog, Poodle, Beagle, Rottweiler, Dachshund, German Shorthaired Pointer, Yorkshire Terrier, Boxer, Siberian Husky, Australian Shepherd, Great Dane, Doberman Pinscher, Miniature Schnauzer, Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, Shih Tzu, Boston Terrier, Pomeranian, Bernese Mountain Dog, Chihuahua, Pug, Akita, Alaskan Malamute, Border Collie, Cocker Spaniel, Cane Corso, Dalmatian, Belgian Malinois, Indian Pariah Dog.

Nutrition

Dogs require a complete and balanced diet appropriate for age, size and activity level, along with clean water.

Common Health Concerns

Dental disease, obesity, skin problems, parasites, gastrointestinal disorders and infectious diseases.

🐈 Cat

A popular companion animal with specialized nutritional and behavioral needs.

Popular Breeds

Persian, Maine Coon, Siamese, Ragdoll, Bengal, British Shorthair, Scottish Fold, Sphynx, Russian Blue, Abyssinian, American Shorthair, Birman, Burmese, Devon Rex, Cornish Rex, Norwegian Forest Cat, Turkish Angora, Turkish Van, Oriental Shorthair, Exotic Shorthair, Savannah, Himalayan, Manx, Somali, Egyptian Mau, Tonkinese, Ragamuffin, Siberian, Bombay, Domestic Shorthair.

Nutrition

Cats need a complete and balanced diet appropriate for their life stage, with adequate protein and water.

Common Health Concerns

Dental disease, obesity, urinary problems, parasites and respiratory infections.

🐄 Cow

Important livestock animal used for dairy, beef and agricultural purposes.

Popular Breeds

Gir, Sahiwal, Red Sindhi, Rathi, Tharparkar, Kankrej, Hariana, Ongole, Deoni, Dangi, Hallikar, Krishna Valley, Kangayam, Vechur, Malnad Gidda, Punganur, Holstein Friesian, Jersey, Brown Swiss, Ayrshire, Guernsey, Simmental, Hereford, Angus, Limousin, Charolais, Shorthorn, Brahman.

Common Health Concerns

Mastitis, foot-and-mouth disease, bloat, metabolic disorders, parasites and reproductive problems.

🐠 Aquarium Fish

Ornamental fish kept in freshwater and marine aquarium systems.

Popular Types

Goldfish, Guppy, Molly, Betta, Tetra, Angelfish and Koi.

Care

Water quality, temperature, filtration, oxygenation and stocking density are important.

Common Health Concerns

🐢 Turtle

Reptile requiring appropriate habitat, temperature and species-specific nutrition.

Popular Breeds

Red-Eared Slider, Yellow-Bellied Slider, Indian Star Tortoise, African Spurred Tortoise, Russian Tortoise, Greek Tortoise, Hermann’s Tortoise, Leopard Tortoise, Sulcata Tortoise, Box Turtle, Painted Turtle, Map Turtle, Musk Turtle, Snapping Turtle, Softshell Turtle, Indian Roofed Turtle, Indian Flapshell Turtle, Asian Forest Turtle.

Care

Appropriate UVB exposure, temperature, habitat hygiene and suitable nutrition.

Common Health Concerns

Shell disease, respiratory disease, nutritional deficiencies and parasites.

Animal Care Basics

Simple principles for responsible animal ownership and management.

💧 Clean Water

Animals should have access to clean, safe drinking water appropriate to their species and environmental conditions.

🥗 Balanced Nutrition

Feeding should be based on species, age, physiological status, activity and production requirements.

🏠 Proper Housing

Housing should provide appropriate space, ventilation, temperature, hygiene and protection.

🧼 Hygiene

Regular cleaning and good hygiene can help reduce the risk of infectious and parasitic disease.

💉 Prevention

Veterinary checkups, vaccination, parasite control and biosecurity are important components of prevention.

❤️ Welfare

Animals should be handled responsibly and provided with appropriate food, shelter, health care and humane treatment.
