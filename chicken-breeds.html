Chicken

NutritionAge-appropriate starter, grower or layer feed, grit/minerals where appropriate and clean water.
Housing / EnvironmentDry, predator-safe coop, ventilation, clean litter and suitable outdoor space.
Preventive Veterinary CareBiosecurity, vaccination programs, parasite control and flock observation.
Common Health ConcernsCoccidiosis, Newcastle disease, infectious bronchitis and parasites.
Typical PurposeEgg, meat, breeding and ornamental uses
ImportantBreed needs can vary by age, sex, workload, climate and individual health status.

For illness, injury, breathing difficulty, severe diarrhea/vomiting, inability to eat/drink, collapse or other urgent signs, contact a veterinarian promptly.
