Rabbit

NutritionUnlimited good-quality hay, suitable greens, measured pellets and fresh water.
Housing / EnvironmentClean, spacious, well-ventilated housing with exercise and safe flooring.
Preventive Veterinary CareDental checks, parasite prevention, nail care and regular weight monitoring.
Common Health ConcernsDental disease, gastrointestinal stasis, parasites and respiratory problems.
Typical PurposeCompanion, meat, fiber and exhibition depending on breed
ImportantBreed needs can vary by age, sex, workload, climate and individual health status.

For illness, injury, breathing difficulty, severe diarrhea/vomiting, inability to eat/drink, collapse or other urgent signs, contact a veterinarian promptly.
